THE CHRISTMAS WORD BAG

Explore the vocabulary

Grouped for classroom use; levels are teaching estimates. Similar words are kept as separate calls. Icons are picture clues and may have more than one interpretation.

Curated from the six supplied Christmas vocabulary and word-search images. Singular/plural variants and synonyms such as gift/present and Santa/Father Christmas are consolidated.

A merry little guide

  1. Choose a level. All the words for the round appear on the page. The teacher can remove words with × and restore them if needed. Students choose nine different words from the remaining list and fill their grids, then the teacher selects “Students ready — start game”.
  2. Choose what to show. Words, icons, or both. You can change this at any time.
  3. Draw a word. Students cross the matching word on their own grid.
  4. Keep drawing. Each new call stays in the sidebar as a written word, including when the main clue uses icons only. Every word is drawn once. Reset starts a new shuffled game using the same word selection and lets you edit the selection again.

A short drumroll plays before each call, bells ring when the word appears, and an English voice reads it aloud. Use Sounds and Voice to mute either, or Repeat word to hear the current call again. When a student shouts bingo, press the Bingo button to play applause; this does not use a microphone. Use “Present to class” for a large, uncluttered display. Press Space for the next call, or Escape to leave presentation view.

Each level remembers its own progress on this browser. Switching levels keeps the existing games. Download the printable template from the sidebar: four blank 3 × 3 cards per A4 page. Before starting, students choose nine different words from the selected level and write one in each square.
